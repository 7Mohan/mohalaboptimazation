## ✨ Features

- **Real tweaks, checked on your phone.** Max refresh rate, faster animations, no window blurs, gaming Do Not Disturb, no pop-up banners, private DNS, faster touch and hold and more. Every tweak is read back after it is applied, and switching it off puts your original setting back.
- **Per-game profiles.** Android's Game Mode, render resolution, frame rate, graphics driver and priority for each game, where Android allows them, plus presets and Focus Launch.
- **MohaLab Boost.** A side panel over your games with live FPS, performance modes, screen recording with game sound, Smart trigger, Net Flash, Do Not Disturb and call blocking.
- **Measured cleaning.** RAM Boost and Ultra Cleaner show what was really freed, never an estimate.
- **Tools.** Device Lab, CPU cores, Touch Claw, touch test, gyro check, Memory Lab, Network Lab and more.
- **Root mode** (Magisk, KernelSU, APatch): CPU and GPU governors and limits, Lag Fighter, network and storage tweaks.
- **Access without root:** through MohaLab Bridge ([github.com/7Mohan/mohalab-bridge](https://github.com/7Mohan/mohalab-bridge)), free from the same developer, or a one-time ADB permission for settings tweaks.
- **9 languages:** English, Indonesian, Portuguese, Filipino, Arabic, Hindi, Malay, Bengali and Vietnamese.

Results depend on the phone and the game. No app can make a phone's chip faster; Moha Lab removes what slows games down.
